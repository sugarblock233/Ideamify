# Code of Conduct

Small project, small ask. This keeps the issue and pull-request spaces useful
for everyone.

## The basics

- **Be respectful.** Critique code, data, and design decisions; do not
  criticize people. Assume good intent in contributions, questions, and
  research claims posted here.
- **Keep it constructive.** "This broke my reproduction with X; here is the
  diff/step that saved me" works. "This is wrong because I said so" does not.
- **No harassment, discrimination, or hateful content** in any form — in
  code, comments, commit messages, issue bodies, PR descriptions, or attached
  data.
- **Keep real research data out.** See the privacy batch in
  `CONTRIBUTING.md`: tokens, private experiments, student data, institutional
  reports do not belong in the repo. If you see posted sensitive content,
  report it per "Reporting" below rather than quoting it further.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests,
discussions, docs, and anywhere contributors act on behalf of the project).
What is acceptable in your own private work is not automatically acceptable
here.

## Reporting

Report conduct violations, or anything you want handled privately (including
sensitive or security-adjacent content, which belongs in `SECURITY.md`), to
the maintainer directly — via a sensitive GitHub issue or the contact routes
documented in `SECURITY.md`. Reports are read seriously and do not need to
match any format.

## Enforcement

Enforcement is at the maintainer's discretion. Possible outcomes range from
an ignored bad-faith post to editing or removing content, temporary or
permanent suspension, or a permanent block. The maintainer may take other
actions appropriate to the circumstances, and may seek advice from others.
If a contribution is rejected on conduct grounds, the maintainer will state
the reason when doing so.
